# I Am an Animal

Humans are animals.  
That is absurd. I'm an animal.

I eat food and stay alive. I sleep. I use my forepaws to do things. I have body hair and legs, and my heart beats. Blood runs through my body. I breathe. I just did it again.

Does that actually feel real to you?
