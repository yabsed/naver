# Sleep

The very existence of sleep can, at first, feel almost like a mockery of me. Seven hours a day without consciousness. Not moving. Not doing anything productive. Consciousness simply cuts out. To think that I am an animal.

But once you truly accept that human beings are finite—that human beings are animals—you realize that there is almost nothing as merciful as sleep. It is okay to do nothing. You are allowed to lie there without thinking, sprawled beneath a warm, soft blanket. And soon enough, you lose even your thoughts. As you should.

What does it matter what happens to me tomorrow? I am going to sleep now. I am not an idea. I am not artificial intelligence. I am an animal built from an ancient, primitive blueprint. I am stupid. Feed me. Love me. Pet me.
