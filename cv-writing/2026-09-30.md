# Pseudo Code 

M_A = author's current world model
M_T = target / conventional / author's local model
P = a claim extracted from M_T
O = observed fact or residual

Extract P from M_T.

Do not caricature P.
Assume the strongest plausible version of P.

Embed P into M_A.

Add constraints:
incentives, costs, time,
biology, institutions, observed behavior.

Simulate.

Compare predicted behavior with O.

If they do not fit:

P may be wrong,
M_A may be incomplete,
a hidden variable may exist,
or the object may be misclassified.
Update whichever model actually failed.

Run again.
